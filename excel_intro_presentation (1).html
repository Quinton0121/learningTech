What is a Spreadsheet?

Before we write formulas, we need to understand the grid. It's simpler than you think.

Press Right Arrow or Click Next to begin

STEP 1: THE STRUCTURE

It's just Columns and Rows.

Columns go down

They are named with letters. Hover here to see Column B.

Rows go across

They are named with numbers. Hover here to see Row 3.

STEP 2: THE ADDRESS

Where they meet is a Cell.

Every box on the screen has a unique name, created by combining its Column Letter and Row Number.

Try it! Type a cell address (like B2 or D3):

Please enter a valid cell between A1 and D3.
STEP 3: THE MAGIC

Cells talk to each other.

Instead of adding numbers together, you add the Cells together. If the numbers inside change, the total updates automatically.
