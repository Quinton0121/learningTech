STEP 1: THE INTERFACE

The 3 Core Windows

Blender's default layout is divided into specialized modular panes. Hover or click each card below to see its exact place and role:

1. 3D Viewport (Center / Main)

Your physical workspace. This is where you create, model, navigate, transform, and view your 3D geometry in real space.

2. Outliner (Top-Right)

The hierarchical scene list (like File Explorer). Shows all objects, collections, cameras, and lights with visibility and render toggles.

3. Properties Editor (Bottom-Right)

The parameters dashboard. Adjust numeric transforms, materials, modifiers, camera focal length, and render engines here.

Pro Tip: Any window corner can be split or joined by dragging the edge!

STEP 3: THE HOLY TRINITY

Moving the Camera

Click a movement type below to see how it affects your perspective.

Orbit

Rotates the camera around the center of your view. Imagine walking in a circle around an object.

Pan

Slides the camera horizontally or vertically, parallel to the screen.

Zoom

Moves the camera closer to or further from the center of focus.

STEP 9: SCALING & PLANES

The Scale Tool & S

The gizmo gives you 3 ways to resize: 1-Axis (Cubes), 2-Axis Plane (Flat Squares), or All 3 Axes (Outer Ring / S).

1-AXIS (CUBE HANDLES):
Red: X Only Green: Y Only Blue: Z Only
FLAT SQUARES = 2D PLANE LOCK:

Scales across two axes at once uniformly while strictly locking the perpendicular 3rd axis.

Blue Square (XY Plane): Scales X & Y | Locks Z (Shift+Z)
Green Square (XZ Plane): Scales X & Z | Locks Y (Shift+Y)
Red Square (YZ Plane): Scales Y & Z | Locks X (Shift+X)

STEP 10: BOUNDING BOX SCALING

The Scale Cage Tool

In standard scaling, objects expand from their center pivot. The Scale Cage gives you a bounding box where dragging a handle scales from the opposite side.

How to Access in Blender:

Long-press the Scale Icon in the left toolbar → choose Scale Cage (or press Shift + Space → Scale Cage).

OPPOSITE ANCHOR BEHAVIOR:

Pull the Top handle upward → The bottom stays firmly on the floor while the top stretches up!
