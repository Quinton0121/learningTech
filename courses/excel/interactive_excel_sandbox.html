Exercise: Revenue Total

Use the grid on the right to calculate the total revenue for our fruit stand. Remember to start all formulas with an equals sign (=).

Your Tasks:

  • Apples Total: In cell D2, multiply the Quantity (B2) by the Price (C2).
    Hint: Type =B2*C2
  • Oranges Total: In cell D4, multiply the Quantity (B4) by the Price (C4).
  • Grand Total: In cell D5, use the SUM function to add up all the totals in column D.
    Hint: Type =SUM(D2:D4)
